1. Is the pressure comfortable today?

Ask for light or moderate pressure and change it whenever needed. A massage should never require you to tolerate pain to “make it work.”

2. What areas will be included?

Agree on the service scope before the session starts. You can ask to avoid the abdomen, lower back or any area that feels sensitive.

3. Which products will be used?

Confirm oils, lotions or aroma products in advance, especially if you have allergies or sensitive skin.

4. Are your symptoms unusual or worrying?

Massage is not a way to diagnose the cause of period pain. If symptoms are severe, unusual or worrying, speak with a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying on a wellness session.
