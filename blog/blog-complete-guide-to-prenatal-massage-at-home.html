Before you book

A pregnancy-aware wellness massage that requires suitable training, positioning and client consent. Ask who will provide the session, whether the requested female therapist is available, and what the complete price includes.

Before the therapist arrives

  • Choose a clean and ventilated room.
  • Keep a clear walking path and enough working space.
  • Decide which areas you want included or avoided.
  • Keep your phone available for arrival coordination but never share OTPs or banking secrets.

At the start of the session

Confirm the service again. The normal focus is gentle pressure and pregnancy-safe positioning by a suitably trained provider. Pressure should be comfortable from the first few minutes and can be changed at any time.

During the session

Speak up if pressure, temperature, position, oil or any part of the service feels uncomfortable. Consent is ongoing, not a one-time permission.

After the session

Take a few minutes to get up slowly and note what pressure level felt comfortable for future bookings. Do not treat a temporary feeling of relaxation as proof that a medical problem has been cured.

Important: Pregnancy massage should only be considered when medically appropriate and with a provider trained for prenatal care. It is not antenatal medical treatment.
