My approach

Not effort problems. The founders who move fastest are the ones who know exactly what the problem is before they start building. I've seen this pattern in Lucknow, in Bengaluru, in Sydney, and in Melbourne.

I work best with founders who value honest direction over comfortable reassurance — regardless of whether they're in India or Australia.
