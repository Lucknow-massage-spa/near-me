Before the appointment

  • Confirm the service, therapist, time, total price and travel charge.
  • Choose a clean, ventilated room with enough working space.
  • Tell the provider about pressure preferences, areas to avoid and important health information.
  • Confirm any oil, lotion or aroma product before it is used.

During the session

The normal focus is relaxation, easy positioning and client-controlled pressure. Pressure should stay comfortable. You can change your mind about any part of the session and ask the therapist to stop.

After the session

Take a few minutes before returning to normal activity. Note which pressure level and setup felt comfortable if you plan another wellness session. Do not treat a temporary feeling of relaxation as a medical diagnosis or cure.
